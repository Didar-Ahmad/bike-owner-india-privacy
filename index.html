Bike Owner India

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

In short: Bike Owner India stores your bike, fuel, trip and reminder information locally on your device. We do not operate a server that collects this information.

Information you enter

You may enter bike names, odometer readings, fuel quantities, fuel expenses, mileage, trip details, loan estimates and maintenance dates. This information is saved locally on your device so the app can provide calculations, history and reminders.

Permissions and notifications

The app may request permission to send notifications. This permission is used only to show maintenance, service, oil-change, insurance or PUC reminders that you create. You can disable notifications at any time in Android settings.

Data collection and sharing

The current version does not require an account and does not collect, sell, rent or share your personal information with the developer or third parties. It does not include advertising, behavioural tracking or analytics SDKs.

Data export

If you choose the export feature, the app creates a JSON backup containing the bike information you entered. The export is initiated and controlled by you. You are responsible for where you save or share that file.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Because the developer does not receive this data, the developer cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

Calculations and financial information

Fuel, trip and EMI results are estimates created from the numbers you enter. The app does not provide loans, process payments, access bank accounts or offer financial advice.

Children's privacy

The app is a general utility for vehicle owners and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data is stored using the app storage provided by Android. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so users should protect access to their device and exported backup files.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact details shown on the Bike Owner India Google Play listing.
